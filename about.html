Our Story

From a Student's Frustration
to a Startup's Purpose

Desire of Knowledge was born from a simple but powerful observation, most coaching centres treat all students the same, regardless of their ability level. Stronger students get bored. Weaker students get lost. Neither truly grows.

As a B.Tech + M.Tech Computer Science graduate, our founder saw this gap and decided to fix it, starting right here with a fresh approach to teaching.

We started with just a handful of students and one big idea: group by ability, not just by class. Place students where they will actually thrive, not where it is convenient.
